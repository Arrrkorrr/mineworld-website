Welcome to MineWorld!

MineWorld is a modded Minecraft server project that is about geopolitics and strategy, created by French students.

MineWorld takes place in a diversed, rich and free-for-all environment, in a modern semi-anarchist sandbox.
You can do everything you want as long as you are not cheating, using automated clients or crashing servers. More information here.

The objective of MineWorld is to offer a free world to Minecraft players in which they can explore a lot of content, organize themselves in nations, manage international relations, make industries, develop their countries, start wars to extend their empires and so on.

We do try to censor the community as less as possible (we still have to comply with local legislation) in order to keep a free environment for all of our players, regardless of their backgrounds and believes, in which they can freely express themselves.

MineWorld proposes popular mods such as Create to make automated farms and industries. However, there are also mods for firearms, missiles, FPV drones, world generation, enhanced and new structures, enhanced and new dimensions, new biomes, new mobs, and many more! Keep in mind that we regularly add new mods and content to explore.

Beside, we also have a Discord server that you may join right now to interact directly with us and the community.
We post annoucements, project news and update notes faster here, and A LOT of memes.

The project costs and servers are entirely paid from our own pockets. We are just a group of friends with a dream, and we are developing this project during our free time, so updates might take time to release. To reduce server costs and support our work, you may donate to us.

Project News

For more details, join our Discord.

Quality of Life Update

The MineColonies and Simple Voice Chat mods have been added to MineWorld to let players talk to each other directly in-game and handle NPC people. We also removed the "Experience" mod pack as it was not really useful.

New Dimension DLC

Explore a brand-new dimension and bosses in MineWorld with the Darker and Deeper mode. We also implemented additional content to the dimension and we updated some mods.

Mod Pack Update

Updated some mods, removed Enlightend that was causing some issues with Create lava suits and added JEI to the Experience mod pack.

Nether and End opening!

The Nether and End dimensions are now opened, with new biomes and structures to explore, mobs to encounter and the Ender Dragon fight was reviewed with the True Ending mod.

FSB v1.6.1

Patched some bugs for the /nation join command.

MineWorld Beta 3.0.1

The Minimal pack was removed, created the Immersion and Experience mod packs, updated some mods, implemented the Sophisticated Backpack mod in order to let players carry more items on themselves, and implemented the TaCZ Automation mod to let players industrialize ammunition production.

FSB v1.6.0

The /nation info command was implemented, fixed some /nation join bug that prevented new nation members to get their role, and moved FSB notifications to the "world" channel.

Mod Pack Updates

Removed Biome Remover and Chunky from the mod packs, removed unnecessary mods to join the server from the Minimal mod pack, and mods were updated.

FSB nation commands

You can now manage your nations directly from Discord with the /nation claim, /nation invite, /nation join, /nation kick, /nation leave, /nation rank, /nation relation commands. You can also make posts using the /journalism command, under your nation flag.
