Welcome to MineWorld!

MineWorld is a modded Minecraft server project that is about geopolitics and strategy, created by French students.

MineWorld takes place in a diversed, rich and free-for-all environment, in a modern semi-anarchist sandbox.
You can do everything you want as long as you are not cheating, using automated clients or crashing servers. More information here.

The objective of MineWorld is to offer a free world to Minecraft players in which they can explore a lot of content, organize themselves in nations, manage international relations, make industries, develop their countries, start wars to extend their empires and so on.

We do try to censor the community as less as possible (we still have to comply with local legislation) in order to keep a free environment for all of our players, regardless of their backgrounds and believes, in which they can freely express themselves.

MineWorld proposes popular mods such as Create to make automated farms and industries. However, there are also mods for firearms, missiles, FPV drones, world generation, enhanced and new structures, enhanced and new dimensions, new biomes, new mobs, and many more! Keep in mind that we regularly add new mods and content to explore.

Beside, we also have a Discord server that you may join right now to interact directly with us and the community.
We post annoucements, project news and update notes faster here, and A LOT of memes.

The project costs and servers are entirely paid from our own pockets. We are just a group of friends with a dream, and we are developing this project during our free time, so updates might take time to release. To reduce server costs and support our work, you may donate to us.
