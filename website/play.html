Join MineWorld today!

You want to play to MineWorld and take on your own journey?
Well, here is how you can join this modern semi-anarchist world.

To join MineWorld, you must create a new 47.4.23 Forge instance for Minecraft 1.20.1.
Then, you must choose and install one of MineWorld mod packs (more information down below) to your newly created instance, and run this modded Minecraft client.
Then add MineWorld to your multiplayer servers list with the IP address 51.75.140.147.

You are generally allowed to add custom client-side mods to your game as long as they do not fall into the "cheating" category.
Coming to optimization mods, MineWorld provides a complete set of mods in order to make your experience as smooth as possible.
You can find more information about server rules here.

MineWorld offers three different mod packs with various use-cases.
We provide tar.gz archives to the community as we believe they are generally better than zip archives and available across all mainstream platforms.

  • The Minimal mod pack offers the basic MineWorld experience by providing required mods ONLY to join and play to the server. You will enjoy all features that the server proposes, without extra unnecessary mods. You can download this mod pack from here.
  • The Immersion mod pack (recommended) offers an enhanced MineWorld experience by providing additional mods to provide a better immersion and feel to the game while playing. It includes tools to install shaders, enhanced sounds, ambient sounds, additional animations and many more. You can download this mod pack from here.
  • The Server mod pack offers the list of mods that are installed server-side for hosting your own private instances of MineWorld with your friends, and also, for transparency with the community coming to what is installed and running on our servers. You can download this mod pack from here.
Keep in mind that you can suggest new features and mods to add to MineWorld, and interact with us and the community by joining our Discord server.
