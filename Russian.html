Добро пожаловать в MineWorld!

MineWorld — это проект модифицированного сервера Minecraft, посвящённый геополитике и стратегии, созданный французскими студентами.

Действие MineWorld разворачивается в разнообразном, богатом и открытом для всех мире — в современной полуанархической «песочнице».
Вы можете делать всё, что захотите, при условии, что не будете обманывать, использовать автоматизированные клиенты или выводить серверы из строя. Подробнеездесь.

Цель MineWorld — предоставить игрокам в Minecraft свободный мир, в котором они смогут исследовать огромное количество контента, объединяться в нации, управлять международными отношениями, создавать промышленные предприятия, развивать свои страны, начинать войны для расширения своих империй и так далее.

Мы стараемся как можно меньше ограничивать свободу сообщества (хотя нам всё же приходится соблюдать местное законодательство), чтобы сохранить свободную среду для всех наших игроков, независимо от их происхождения и убеждений, в которой они могут свободно выражать свои мысли.

MineWorld предлагает такие популярные моды, как Create, позволяющие создавать автоматизированные фермы и промышленные объекты. Однако есть также моды, посвящённые огнестрельному оружию, ракетам, дронам с FPV-управлением, генерации миров, усовершенствованным и новым сооружениям, усовершенствованным и новым измерениям, новым биомам, новым мобам и многому другому! Имейте в виду, что мы регулярно добавляем новые моды и контент, который можно исследовать.

Кроме того, у нас также есть сервер в Discord где вы можете зарегистрироваться прямо сейчас, чтобы напрямую общаться с нами и с сообществом.
Здесь мы быстрее публикуем объявления, новости о проектах и заметки об обновлениях, а также ОЧЕНЬ МНОГО мемов.

Расходы на проект и серверы мы полностью покрываем из собственных средств. Мы — просто группа друзей с мечтой, и разрабатываем этот проект в свободное от работы время, поэтому выпуск обновлений может занять некоторое время. Чтобы сократить расходы на серверы и поддержать нашу работу, вы можете сделать пожертвование нам.

Новости проекта

Чтобы узнать больше, присоединяйтесь к нашему Discord.

Additional Content

We added the MineColonies mod to MineWorld in order to let players handle their nations, Simple Voice Chat to implement a proximity chat to MineWorld, and the Immersive Vehicles mod in order to implement cars, planes, fuel pumps, signs and many more to the server.

New Dimension DLC

Explore a brand-new dimension and bosses in MineWorld with the Darker and Deeper mode. We also implemented additional content to the dimension and we updated some mods.

Mod Pack Update

Updated some mods, removed Enlightend that was causing some issues with Create lava suits and added JEI to the Experience mod pack.

Nether and End opening!

The Nether and End dimensions are now opened, with new biomes and structures to explore, mobs to encounter and the Ender Dragon fight was reviewed with the True Ending mod.

FSB v1.6.1

Patched some bugs for the /nation join command.

MineWorld Beta 3.0.1

The Minimal pack was removed, created the Immersion and Experience mod packs, updated some mods, implemented the Sophisticated Backpack mod in order to let players carry more items on themselves, and implemented the TaCZ Automation mod to let players industrialize ammunition production.

FSB v1.6.0

The /nation info command was implemented, fixed some /nation join bug that prevented new nation members to get their role, and moved FSB notifications to the "world" channel.

Mod Pack Updates

Removed Biome Remover and Chunky from the mod packs, removed unnecessary mods to join the server from the Minimal mod pack, and mods were updated.

FSB nation commands

You can now manage your nations directly from Discord with the /nation claim, /nation invite, /nation join, /nation kick, /nation leave, /nation rank, /nation relation commands. You can also make posts using the /journalism command, under your nation flag.

Beta 3.0 COMING SOON

MineWorld will officially come back on 29.08.2026 on the Forge platform with a crazy amount of new features to explore and enjoy! A complete and long update note has been made in the annoucement channel of our Discord server.

The project has been entirely moved to Paper, on a brand new server that has a better network and better components. Ashbound Event

This is the END OF THE WORLD! As it is the end of Beta 1.0, we decided to pass the server into full anarchy and let players do whatever they want. The spawn has been transformed into an illegal items shop where you can find impossible enchants, items and mass destruction weapons.

End of Build Contest

You can now vote for your favorite builds in the "polls" channel on our Discord server.

Server Meeting #2

The meeting was attended by 4 people and we discussed about server rules, hosting services, development and Beta 2.0. You can participate in the Forge vs Paper debate on our Discord server.

Server Reset

The Nether, End and Mining Dimensions were entirely reset with a new seed! The desert theme for the Mining Dimension is over.

Server Update

We reset some regions of the world that were still irradiated due to the nuclear conflicts that occurred in the region. We reset the Nether and End dimensions and changed the seed as expected, adjusted some settings for the economy mod, increased the amount of homes, and banned some illegal items and nukes from the auction house.

Mining Dimension Fixes

Implemented daylight cycle and rain in the mining dimension, set the dimension as an overworld-like dimension, enabled anti X-Ray for the dimension as well.

Factions and Claims Update

Removed the Brimm Armors, Easy Factions and JourneyMap mods and replaced them by FTB Teams and FTB chunks for better performance and reduce network issues that was preventing some players from joining the world. Note that all factions and claims no longer exist :).

Anti X-Ray Update

Some unnecessary mods have ben removed from the client-side mod pack. We also implemented an anti X-Ray to prevent some known players from cheating. The mining dimension is now like the overworld and will also be reset every week to prevent bases from being made here. We also increased the amount of homes.

Build Contest

Here is a new server event! Build some famous places and monuments from your nation to get various rewards. Conditions and awards are present in the annoucement channel of our Discord server. Griefs are not allowed!

End of Resource Race

Congratulations to Russia that brought the most Nether Stars, and to Brazil for retrieveing the Ender Dragon egg!

Mod Pack Update

Updated some mods to their latest versions available, and removed the Simple Voice Chat mod that was not working.

Resource Race

Farm as much Nether Stars as possible! The nations that bring back the most Nether Stars will receive some rewards. As well, the End Portal will be opened and its coordinates will be revealed. The nation that brings the Ender Dragon egg back will receive a NUCLEAR BOMB!

Server Meeting #1

The meeting was attended by 5 people and we discussed about server performance issues, installed mods (what we should keep or not), known problems, the FSB bot and how to increase MineWorld player base.

Performance and Stability Patch

Increased the RAM allocation to the server and increased server swap to prevent Java heap space crashes as much as possible in the future.

Features Removal

We deactivated some commands from FTB Essentials that were not useful or overpowered. We also removed some unused mods from the mod pack, this should increase your performance in game.

Server Launch

MineWorld Beta 1.0 is finally releasing after weeks of preparations. All information on how to join the server are available in the annoucement channels of our Discord server.
